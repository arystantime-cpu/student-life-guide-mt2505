Study at AITU with confidence

Understand the academic calendar, credits, syllabus and grading system.

Academic calendar

One trimester usually contains ten study weeks followed by final exams.

Weeks 1-4

Classes and assignments.

Week 5

Midterm exams.

Weeks 6-9

Classes and assignments.

Week 10

Endterm exams.

Final exams

Exams according to the schedule.

How your grade works

Register Midterm

30% of the total grade.

Register Endterm

30% of the total grade.

Final exam

40% of the total grade.

Total grade

Midterm × 0.3 + Endterm × 0.3 + Final × 0.4

Credits

A credit shows the academic weight of a course. The number of credits is connected to the weekly workload.

You can find the credit value of each course in the Curriculum section of Digital University.

Syllabus

A syllabus explains the course topics, assignments and assessment system.

Read the evaluation section to understand which tasks have the greatest effect on your grade.

Course requirements

  • Attend classes regularly.
  • Submit assignments before their deadlines.
  • Complete the midterm, endterm and final exam.
  • Check the requirements in each course syllabus.

Remember

Assessment details can be different for each course. Always use the current syllabus and information from your instructor.
