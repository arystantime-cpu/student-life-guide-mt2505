Learn how campus blocks, floors and classroom numbers work.

Three campus blocks

All three blocks are inside one building and are connected by stairs.

C1.1

This block is located near the library.

C1.2

This is the central block near the main entrance.

C1.3

This block is located near the canteen.

How to read a room number

Example: C1.3.240

  • C1 is the building.
  • 3 is the block.
  • 2 is the floor.
  • 240 is the room number.

Room letters

  • L means lecture room.
  • P means practice room.
  • K means computer room.
